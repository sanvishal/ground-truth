# Level 1 audio sources

These runtime files were derived from audio supplied by the project owner on 2026-08-30.

- `spark-01.mp3` through `spark-03.mp3`: `faespencer-high-voltage-spark-486895.mp3`
- `steam-01.mp3` through `steam-04.mp3`: `freesound_community-steam-sfx-26907.mp3`
- `alarm-loop.mp3`: `emir3427-alarm-478339.mp3`
- `ambient-space.mp3`: `luceris-space-590393.mp3`
- `panel-open.mp3`: `dragon-studio-opening-door-sfx-454240.mp3`
- `panel-close.mp3`: `dragon-studio-door-closed-382707.mp3`
- `panel-error.mp3`: `freesound_community-error-sound-39539.mp3`

The spark and steam recordings were split into short runtime variations, faded, loudness-normalized, converted to 44.1 kHz MP3, and level-balanced in code. The alarm and ambient recordings were normalized and prepared as loops.
The panel recordings had their silent tails removed and received short output fades.
The panel error recording had its silent tail removed and received a short output fade.
